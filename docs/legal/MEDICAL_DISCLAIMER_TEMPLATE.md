## Disclaimer Statement

The ROM Platform ("Service") provides computer-vision-assisted musculoskeletal range-of-motion measurement and clinical documentation support. **This Service is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.**

## Key Limitations

1. **Not a Diagnostic Tool:** The Service provides measurement assistance only. It does not provide diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical interpretations.

2. **Clinician Review Required:** All measurement outputs, generated notes, and exported documents **must be reviewed and validated by a qualified licensed clinician** before any clinical use, documentation, or patient communication.

3. **Measurement Accuracy:** Computer vision measurements are approximations. Confidence scores and quality flags are provided to assist clinicians in evaluating measurement reliability. Low-confidence measurements should be verified through manual goniometry.

4. **Clinical Judgment Prevails:** In any case where Service outputs conflict with clinical judgment or manual examination findings, the clinician's professional assessment takes precedence.

5. **Not Emergency Use:** The Service is not designed for emergency or acute care situations.

## Intended Use

- Documentation support for routine musculoskeletal range-of-motion assessments
- Progress tracking across clinical sessions
- Clinical note generation for review and finalization by licensed clinicians
