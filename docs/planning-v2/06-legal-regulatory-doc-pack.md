# 06 - Legal and Regulatory Document Pack

This is a planning document, not legal advice.

## Regulatory decision point

You must choose one initial posture:

1. Documentation-assist product with conservative claims
2. Medical-device trajectory (SaMD) with formal regulatory pathway

## Required legal artifacts (pre-launch baseline)

- Terms of Service (clinician organizations)
- Privacy Policy (HIPAA-aware + jurisdiction clauses)
- Business Associate Agreement template (if handling PHI for covered entities)
- Data Processing Addendum (for relevant jurisdictions)
- Medical/clinical disclaimer language for UI and exports
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Incident notification policy

## Regulatory workstreams (if SaMD path chosen)

- Intended use statement
- Risk management file (ISO 14971 aligned)
- Software lifecycle plan (IEC 62304 aligned)
- Clinical evaluation strategy
- Post-market surveillance plan

## Contracting and liability controls

- Explicit limitation of liability and indemnity boundaries
- Responsibility split between platform and clinician judgment
- Audit rights and data handling clauses for enterprise customers

## Consent and patient communication

- Patient consent workflow for capture and processing
- Clear disclosure of AI-assisted measurement nature
- Data retention and deletion rights disclosure

## Legal review gates

- Gate 1: Legal counsel review before pilot contracts
- Gate 2: Legal + compliance sign-off before production go-live
- Gate 3: Quarterly legal review for policy/version updates

## Disclaimer

This document is a template for informational purposes. Consult with a qualified attorney for legal advice specific to your situation.
