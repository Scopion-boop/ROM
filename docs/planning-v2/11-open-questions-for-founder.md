# 11 - Open Questions for Founder (Decision Log Inputs)

## Product and market

1. Initial target market: US only, UK/EU, or mixed?
2. Initial buyer: solo clinics, group practices, or hospitals?
3. Pricing preference: per clinician seat, per clinic, or hybrid?

## Clinical and regulatory

4. Is V1 marketed as documentation-assist only, or do you want explicit clinical measurement claims?
5. What minimum accuracy target do you want for V1 acceptance?
6. Which three joints must be in V1 without compromise?

## Platform strategy

7. Should V1 be web-first only, or include native mobile from day one?
8. Is dual-camera support required in V1 or deferred to V2?
9. Do you want mandatory clinician review for every generated note in V1?

## Data and privacy

10. Should raw videos be stored at all, or process-then-discard by default?
11. What is your retention policy target for session records?
12. Any jurisdictions with strict data residency requirements at launch?

## Operations and rollout

13. Do you want pilot-first at 1-3 clinics, or immediate broader rollout?
14. What support SLA do you want to promise pilot customers?
15. What is your go/no-go date target for first pilot launch?

## Team and execution

16. How many concurrent AI coding agents do you want active at once?
17. Do you want strict human approval before every merge, or only for high-risk areas?
18. Which tool should be your source-of-truth hub (GitHub Projects, Linear, Jira, Notion)?

## Commercial/legal

19. Do you already have access to healthcare legal counsel for policy/regulatory review?
20. Do you have existing partner clinics willing to provide validation data?
